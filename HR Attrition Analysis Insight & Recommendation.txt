1) Gender Distribution
Insight

The workforce consists of 60% male employees and 40% female employees.

This reflects a moderately male-dominated workforce.

Recommendation

Continue promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion by ensuring equal opportunities in recruitment, promotion, and professional development.

2) Department Distribution
Insight

The Research & Development department accounts for approximately 65% of the workforce, making it the organization's largest department.

Sales represents approximately 30%, while Human Resources accounts for about 4%.

Recommendation

Because Research & Development represents the largest workforce segment, management should prioritize employee engagement, career 
development, and workload management within this department to reduce turnover and maintain productivity.

3) Workforce Size
Insight

The organization employs 1,470 employees, providing a large workforce across Human Resources, Sales, and Research & Development.

Recommendation

Management should continue monitoring workforce trends to ensure staffing levels remain aligned with business growth and operational demands.
