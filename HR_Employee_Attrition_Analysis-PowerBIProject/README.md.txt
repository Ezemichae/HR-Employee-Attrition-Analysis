PROJECT REVIEW

This project analyzes employee attrition using Power BI. The objective is to identify workforce trends, understand factors influencing employee turnover, and provide actionable recommendations to improve employee retention.

MANAGEMENT QUESTIONS

1. Which departments have the highest employee attrition?
2. Does overtime increase employee attrition?
3. Which job roles earn the highest salaries?
4. Which age group has the highest employee attrition?
5. Does job satisfaction influence employee attrition?

GENERAL PROJECT INSIGHTS

Executive Summary

INSIGHTS

The organization has 1,470 employees, with an attrition rate of 16.12% (237 employees left).
The average employee age is 37 years, indicating a predominantly mid-career workforce.
Research & Development is the largest department.
The workforce is approximately 60% male and 40% female.

REOMMENDATIONS

Prioritize retention strategies in high-attrition departments.
Continue workforce planning to maintain a balanced age distribution.
Track attrition trends regularly using executive dashboards.

EMPLOYEES DEMOGRAPHICS

INSIGHTTS 

Male employees make up approximately 60% of the workforce.
Employees aged 30–39 years represent the largest workforce segment.
Most employees are married.
Life Sciences is the most common education field.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Maintain diversity and inclusion initiatives.
Develop targeted career programs for employees in the 30–39 age group.
Tailor learning and development initiatives by education background.
Compensation & Career Growth

INSIGHTS

Managers earn the highest average monthly salary.
Research & Development has the highest department salary.
Most employees have worked 0–5 years.
Most employees completed 2–3 training sessions during the previous year.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Continue benchmarking salaries against market standards.
Increase training participation among employees with little or no training.
Review promotion opportunities for long-tenured employees.

ATTRITION DRIVERS

INSIGHTS

Employees working overtime have higher attrition.
Lower job satisfaction is associated with increased attrition.
Employees who travel frequently show higher attrition than non-travelers.
Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives experience higher employee turnover.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Reduce excessive overtime through better workload management.
Improve employee engagement and recognition programs.
Review travel policies to reduce employee fatigue.
Conduct stay interviews and targeted retention initiatives for high-turnover job roles.
