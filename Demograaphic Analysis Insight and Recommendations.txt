1) Age Group Distribution
Insight

The largest proportion of employees falls within the 30–39 years age group, indicating that the organization relies heavily on mid-career professionals.

Recommendation
Invest in leadership development and career progression opportunities for employees in this age group.
Strengthen retention initiatives since this group also experiences the highest attrition in your overall analysis.

2) Education Field Distribution
Insight

Employees with Life Sciences and Medical educational backgrounds make up the majority of the workforce, while Human Resources represents the smallest educational field.

Recommendation
Continue developing technical and professional training programs tailored to employees from Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds.
Diversify recruitment where appropriate to broaden the organization's talent base.

3) Marital Status Distribution
Insight

Most employees are Married, followed by Single, with Divorced employees making up the smallest group.

Recommendation
Continue supporting flexible work arrangements and employee wellness initiatives that benefit employees with different family and personal responsibilities.

Monitor whether retention strategies should differ across marital status groups.


4) Gender Distribution
Insight

The workforce consists of approximately 60% male employees and 40% female employees, indicating a moderately male-dominated workforce.

Recommendation
Continue promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion through fair recruitment, promotion, and career development practices.
Monitor workforce diversity metrics over time to support balanced representation.
