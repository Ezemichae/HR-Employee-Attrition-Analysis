1) Average Monthly Salary by Job Role
Insight

Managers earn the highest average monthly salary, followed by Research Directors. Sales Executives and Healthcare Representatives earn comparatively lower average salaries.

Recommendation
Continue reviewing salary structures to ensure compensation remains competitive.
Evaluate whether lower-paid job roles have sufficient opportunities for career progression and performance-based rewards.
Conduct regular market benchmarking to maintain equitable pay across comparable roles.

2) Average Monthly Salary by Department
Insight

Research & Development has the highest average monthly salary, followed closely by Sales and Human Resources.

Recommendation
Regularly assess salary competitiveness across departments to ensure pay aligns with market rates, job complexity, and employee performance.
Monitor internal pay equity to support fairness and improve employee retention.

3) Employees by Training Sessions Completed
Insight

Most employees completed two to three training sessions during the previous year, while relatively few participated in five or more.

Recommendation
Expand access to learning and development programs.
Encourage employees to participate in additional training aligned with career goals.
Measure the impact of training on performance and retention to ensure training investments deliver value.

4) Employee Distribution by Years at Company
Insight

A large proportion of employees have less than ten years of service, while relatively few have long tenure.

Recommendation
Strengthen onboarding, mentoring, and early-career support to improve retention during employees' first years.
Recognize and reward long-serving employees to encourage loyalty and reduce turnover.

5) Employees by Years Since Last Promotion
Insight

Most employees received a promotion within the past zero to two years, while a smaller number have experienced longer periods without advancement.

Recommendation
Review promotion policies to ensure employees have transparent career progression opportunities.
Conduct regular career development discussions with employees who have gone several years without promotion to reduce disengagement and turnover.
